# Screenshot shot-list

Play needs 2 to 8 phone screenshots (16:9 to 9:16, min 320 px, max 3840 px on a side), a 512x512 icon, and a
1024x500 feature graphic. Add 7-inch and 10-inch tablet sets (recommended for large-screen and foldable ranking).
Capture from a release build, light theme first, then repeat the first three in dark.

Rules: no gambling-themed game in the first six slots (keeps the listing family-friendly under either route in
FAMILIES-CHECKLIST.md); no real names (use "Ana", "Sam", "Mia"); no third-party trademarks in the frame (hide
the UNO and Yahtzee titles until renamed); text size at default; device status bar clean (demo mode).

| # | Screen | What to show | Caption (EN / ES) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Game Hub home | The game grid with families (board, card, puzzle, party) | "Dozens of games, one app" / "Decenas de juegos, una sola app" |
| 2 | Profile picker | Three profiles (adult, two kids) with avatars | "A profile for everyone" / "Un perfil para cada uno" |
| 3 | Kids home | Child profile home with big game tiles | "Games chosen for their age" / "Juegos elegidos para su edad" |
| 4 | Parent dashboard | Daily limit, quiet hours, weekly play-time bars | "You set the limits" / "Tú pones los límites" |
| 5 | A board game mid-match | Chess or checkers with last-move highlight | "Classics with a friendly computer" / "Clásicos contra un rival amable" |
| 6 | A party game | Draw and guess or tap race, two players on one screen | "Pass the device, share the fun" / "Pasa el móvil y juega en compañía" |
| 7 | Puzzle | Sudoku or flow lines, with a hint shown | "Puzzles that grow with you" / "Puzles que crecen contigo" |
| 8 | Nearby lobby | Seat list with two named players, no names of real people | "Play with friends nearby" / "Juega con amigos cercanos" |

Tablet and foldable set: slots 1, 5, 6 in landscape on the tablet; slot 1 and 5 on the Fold inner screen and
tabletop posture if the layout supports it. Wear OS/TV: not applicable.

Capture method: the owner or a device-testing session captures these on a real device (this release team
installed nothing and used no device). Keep original PNGs outside git (store art does not belong in the repo
unless the owner wants it in `docs/release/art/`).

Feature graphic (1024x500): the GameSuite name over a row of five tile icons from different families; reuse
in-app art only, no stock imagery.
