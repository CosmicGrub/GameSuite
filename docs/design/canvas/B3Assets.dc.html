Consistency rules
One Blender template, one material set, one lighting rig (warm key, cool rim, fill).
Bevel 1 step, flat shading, accent glow = function.
Cool accents = ours, warm rock = theirs.
Scripts: renders/make_models.py, scene_pass.py.
Proof facts
Blender MCP unreachable (localhost:9876 refused); installed Blender 5.1.2 run headless, nothing installed.
Whole proof set: five GLBs, 147 KB total, 1 to 2 s per render, about 300 lines of Python.
Haze, Tender, Aegis, Darter, Plater, Splitter, Courier and Behemoth are specified in the asset bible, not yet modelled.
