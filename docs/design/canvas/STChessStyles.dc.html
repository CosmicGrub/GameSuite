Redlines: Chess

Numbers match the dots on the boards.
1
Board Flat: two theme tokens, no texture. 2.5D: 50 degree tilt, 0.3 square slab edge, no perspective. 3D: 57 degree camera with mild perspective, walnut and maple grain (one 512 px texture).M
2
Pieces Flat: 6 silhouette paths per colour. 2.5D: same paths plus a hard two-tone split. 3D: 5 lathe profiles and 1 knight mesh, 24 segments, instanced.L
3
Last move and hints Amber tint on from and to squares, dark dots for legal moves. Identical in all styles so hints never depend on art.S
4
Shadows 2.5D: two stacked ellipses baked as sprites, shrink 20 percent while lifted. 3D: soft contact shadow plus one directional light, no real-time shadow map.M
5
Soft lighting One key light from upper left and a 0.35 vignette. No bloom. Pieces share one gradient per colour.M
6
Move arc Standard: 320 ms, cubic-bezier(.3,0,.2,1), lift peak 1.0 square. Calm slides with no lift. Lively lifts 1.3 and lands with a 60 ms squash.M
7
Capture Flat: victim shrinks and fades in 160 ms. 2.5D: pops up, drops to the tray. 3D: scripted tumble, no physics. Lively adds a dust ring and a 40 ms hit pause. Calm cross-fades.M
