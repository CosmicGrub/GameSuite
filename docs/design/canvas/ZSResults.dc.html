Unlocks and feedback
- Maps unlock by total stars (2 / 4 / 6 / ... / 21), never by money.
- Level-up beats: stars fly to the total, a map tile pops open.
- Haptics: win = rising double tap; defeat = one long soft pulse; new high score = tick tick.
- Audio: original synth stings; victory 2.5 s, defeat 1.8 s (see SPEC).
- Defeat card names the leaking enemy type and lane, and offers one hint.
- Kids: Cadet defeat copy is gentle ('So close! Try a Tender near the entry.').
- Local board holds the top 10 per map and tier; online boards later behind a parent-controlled toggle.
