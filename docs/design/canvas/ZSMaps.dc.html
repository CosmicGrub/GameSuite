Bot Bastion: twelve original arenas in four sectors
Each map: lanes (glowing), build nodes (dashed circles), the Core, red entry rings, optional void hazards. 16:9 arena; coordinates in MAPS.md. Layouts are our own geometry.
1. Hearth Orbitsector 1: Cradle Reach
1 lane, 14 nodes. One long spiral lane. The tutorial map. 14 nodes, about 30 s end to end, unlock: 0 stars.
2. Twin Gatessector 1: Cradle Reach
2 lanes merge. Two entries merge before the core. Put the heavy guns at the merge. 16 nodes, about 23 s end to end, unlock: 0 stars.
3. Pebble Ringsector 1: Cradle Reach
1 lane, full orbit. A single lane that orbits the core before it dives in. 18 nodes, about 30 s end to end, unlock: 2 stars.
4. Crosswindsector 2: Glass Belt
4 lanes, all converge. Four entries. Nowhere is safe, so split your bots. 18 nodes, about 16 s end to end, unlock: 4 stars.
5. Long Wakesector 2: Glass Belt
1 lane, 5 passes. A serpent of a lane. Enemies cross your kill zone again and again. 22 nodes, about 44 s end to end, unlock: 6 stars.
6. Rift Splitsector 2: Glass Belt
split and rejoin, void hazard. The lane splits around a void rift and rejoins at the core. 18 nodes, about 30 s end to end, unlock: 8 stars.
7. Furnacesector 3: Ember Deep
1 lane, spiral 1.7 turns. Two turns of spiral. The slowest route in the game. 20 nodes, about 30 s end to end, unlock: 10 stars.
8. Pinwheelsector 3: Ember Deep
4 curved lanes. Four curved arms spin into the core. 20 nodes, about 22 s end to end, unlock: 12 stars.
9. Hourglasssector 3: Ember Deep
3 lanes funnel to a waist. Three entries squeeze through one narrow waist. 18 nodes, about 23 s end to end, unlock: 14 stars.
10. Tidal Locksector 4: Hush Verge
6 lanes, rim loop. Six straight lanes. The rim patrol (Skiff) shines here. 20 nodes, about 16 s end to end, unlock: 16 stars.
11. Lantern Mazesector 4: Hush Verge
2 interleaved lanes. Two lanes cross over the same ground at different times. 20 nodes, about 30 s end to end, unlock: 18 stars.
12. The Last Lanternsector 4: Hush Verge
3 lanes, boss lane. The finale: three lanes, a boss on every tenth wave. 22 nodes, about 25 s end to end, unlock: 21 stars.
