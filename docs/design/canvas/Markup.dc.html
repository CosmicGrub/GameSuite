Markup and specs

Red numbered dots on the screens point to the notes below. The table maps each audit finding to the screen that answers it.
1
Home: resume firstHero card 28 radius, 20 padding, min height 196. Resume is the only saffron button on screen. Shows a live mini board; tap anywhere on the card also resumes.
2
Home: daily rowThree equal cards, 12 padding, 36 icon chips. Done state turns the chip mint and adds a tick, never colour alone. Counts toward the streak.
3
Setup: pinned Start56 high, 28 radius, always above the gesture bar, with a fade behind it. Fixes the unreachable Start button at 1.6x text in landscape.
4
Board tableBoard is square, 16 horizontal margin, fills the width. Last move tinted saffron 35%, legal moves are dots, pieces carry an outline so they read on both square colours.
5
Action dockFour 56 dp round buttons with text labels under each. Hint is the only filled one. Undo and Hint leave the overflow menu. 12 sp labels scale with text size.
6
ResultOne celebration, one primary action (Rematch). Confetti 24 shapes, double haptic pulse. Reduced Motion: static trophy, no confetti, no pulse.
7
Kids: grown-ups lock56 dp lock in the corner. A short tap does nothing, so it cannot be hit by accident; the parent gate opens on a press-and-hold of 1.5 s.
8
Kids targets and voiceTiles 150 by 176, text 22 sp rounded type, one idea per screen. A speaker button re-reads the instruction. Pre-readers play by icon and sound.
