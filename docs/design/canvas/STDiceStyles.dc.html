Redlines: Dice

Numbers match dots on the boards.
1
Face art Flat: ivory face, 1 to 6 pips, no outline weight change. 2.5D: same faces on a 36 degree tilt with a hard dark side. 3D: rounded cube, key light upper left, pips as decals so they stay crisp.M
2
Tumble Real rotation, three random axes seeded by the result so the die always ends on the rolled face. No physics engine: scripted keyframes plus a seeded spin.M
3
Settle bounce Contact at 470 ms squashes to 0.9 height for 60 ms, then one small rebound. Shadow tightens and darkens as the die falls.S
4
Calm Slide in and rotate 90 degrees, 350 ms, ease out. No bounce, no particles, no shake. Used by Reduced Motion.S
5
Standard 900 ms, two bounces, 6 dust specks at first contact. Haptic tick on each contact.S
6
Lively 1300 ms, three bounces, squash 0.9, 14 sparks, extra flash on doubles or a Yahtzee. Held dice keep a 0.45 lift and an amber ring.M
7
Game speed Slow multiplies time by 1.5. Fast by 0.6 and drops the third bounce. Result is readable at 200 ms even on Fast.S
