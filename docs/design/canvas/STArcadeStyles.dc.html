Redlines: arcade styles

Numbers match dots on the boards.
1
Flat table Pale ice rink, red centre line, dark goal slots, solid mallets. No gradients, so it is also the high contrast base.S
2
Neon arcade Dark navy, lines drawn twice: a crisp 2.5 dp stroke over a blurred glow. Puck has a 5-frame trail at Standard. Calm removes the trail and cuts glow by half.M
3
3D table Pitch 36, mild perspective, glossy ice, cylinder mallets, puck reflection as a faded mirror copy (no reflection pass), contact shadow.L
4
Flat lane and board Top-down lane with straight boards and circle pins. Darts: flat segment wheel, dart as a line with a tip.S
5
2.5D lane and board Lane tilted 52 degrees, pins white with a red band and hard shadow. Board has a wooden rim and 5 dp drop shadow.M
6
3D lane and board Lane pitch 26 with perspective, maple grain, pins as lathe silhouettes. Dart is a barrel with a flight, board has a soft sheen.L
7
Camera follow Camera tracks the ball at 0.35 of its speed after release, and cuts to a 0.6 s pin close-up. Reduced Motion: no tracking, fixed camera.M
8
Strike and bullseye Calm: pins and dart simply appear changed, 200 ms. Standard: pins scatter 500 ms. Lively: scatter 800 ms, 12 sparks, 120 ms hit pause.M
9
Budget rule Anything above 8.3 ms on a 120 Hz panel is capped to 60 Hz for that game. The player never sees a stutter, only a lower refresh.S
10
Budget chart Bars are targets, not measurements. Verify on the Fold 5 and the reference phone before the pilot.S
