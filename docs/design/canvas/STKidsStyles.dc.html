Redlines: kid styles

Numbers match dots on the boards.
1
Storybook wobble Ink outline 2.6 dp, edges displaced by a 4 dp noise map baked into sprites, so it never shimmers on scroll. Pastel fills, Baloo numerals.M
2
Ladder and slide Ladders and slides stay marked by shape in every style: rungs for ladders, a wide curved ribbon for slides. Colour is never the only cue.S
3
Paper craft Every piece is a cut-out with a 3 dp offset shadow and an inner lighter layer. No blur, so it stays cheap and crisp.M
4
Token hop Standard: 300 ms, arc 26 dp, squash 0.86 on crouch and 0.9 on land. Calm: slide with no arc. Lively: arc 36 dp, 60 ms hold, extra squash.M
5
Toy 3D Rounded blocks with a 9 dp front bevel, glossy tokens on a peg, soft contact shadows. The most tactile skin, kept to one light.L
6
Bridge Builders look Water is a scribble in Storybook, three layered paper waves in Paper craft, a flat bright pool in Toy 3D. Planks keep their 72 dp hit areas.S
7
Plank place Lowers 200 ms, lands 320 ms with one small rebound (Lively two), rope tie shows a 6-spark twinkle at Standard. Calm: fade in place.M
8
Calm celebration One star stamps in, no bits, 400 ms. Also what Reduced Motion shows.S
9
Standard celebration Star pops 1.08, 10 bits, 1.2 s, soft glow.S
10
Lively celebration Star bounces 1.22, 24 bits, 2 s, 3 soft haptic pulses, never flashing faster than 3 per second.M
Fallback rule, kids
Toy 3Dfalls back toPaperfalls back toFlat
Toy 3D falls back to Paper craft, then to the flat Kids skin, on the shared trigger: p95 over 14 ms for 90 frames, battery saver, Severe thermal, or under 4 GB RAM.
A child never sees an error or a toast about it; a parent sees it in Settings.
