Redlines: Cards

Numbers match dots on the boards.
1
Flat faces Ivory face, 4 suit paths, corner index 27 percent of card width. Colour deck uses a white diamond badge, a number in the card colour and two corner numbers. No suit or colour is the only cue: UNO-style cards also carry the number and a pattern in the high-contrast pack.S
2
2.5D fan Cards rotate 12 degrees apart around a pivot below the screen. Two stacked shadow rects, no blur. The tapped card lifts 14 dp and straightens.M
3
3D table Cards are 0.05 thick boxes, hand fan leans back 14 degrees. One soft blurred shadow per card on the felt, one key light. Thickness only shows at flip and deal.L
4
Deal 360 ms, arc lift 1.3 cards, spin 30 degrees, cubic-bezier(.3,0,.2,1). Calm: slide only, no lift. Lively: 440 ms with a 4 percent overshoot on landing.M
5
Flip 320 ms, scaleX in flat and 2.5D, true Y rotation in 3D. Face swaps at 90 degrees. Calm: 160 ms cross-fade, no rotation.M
6
Calm shower No falling cards. Rim glow on the winning hand and a 400 ms banner cross-fade. This is also the Reduced Motion result.S
7
Standard shower 14 instanced quads, 1.2 s, gravity easing, no spin, single warm glow behind the hand.S
8
Lively shower 36 quads with spin and motion streaks, 2 s, glow 84 dp, banner overshoot. Still one instanced draw call.M
