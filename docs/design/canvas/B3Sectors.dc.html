Sector design rules
Palette lock: ground, lane, key, rim, accent per sector (see ASSET-BIBLE.md).
Lane strip is always the lowest-detail, highest-contrast band (>= 3:1 against ground, checked in CVD filters).
Cool accents stay ours, warm rock stays theirs in every sector; Ember uses amber accents but keeps rock-red eyes distinct by silhouette.
Props sit on the rim and in dead zones: never within 0.25 u of a lane or pad, never taller than 1.2 u, never in the bottom 1.5 u (occlusion).
All backdrops and props are original geometry; no stock models.
