Rules sheet (opened from the info button)
Campaign rules
1
Goal. Survive 10 waves. The Royal Castle starts with 20 health; each leaked enemy costs 1 (a boss costs 5).
2
Build. Place towers anywhere buildable. Enemies always keep a route; a blocked ghost turns red and tells you why.
3
Gold. Start gold by difficulty (Gentle 200, Easy 150, Medium 100, Hard 70). Kills pay gold; calling a wave early adds a bonus.
4
Stars. 1 for winning, 2 for finishing with 50% castle health or more, 3 for no leaks at all.
5
Pause. You can place, move, upgrade and sell while paused. Leaving mid-run counts the furthest wave.
