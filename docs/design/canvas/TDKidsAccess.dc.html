What TalkBack says
Cell G4: "Row 4, column G. Empty, buildable. Route passes 2 tiles east. Double tap to place Archer Post, 50 gold."
Tower: "Mage Spire, level 3 of 5, Control. Targeting First. Actions: upgrade 140, sell 85, move, change targeting."
Enemy: "Brute, 60% health, 4 tiles from the castle."
Wave: "Wave 6 of 10 started, 14 enemies. Castle 17 of 20."
Alternative: a list view (Towers, Enemies, Free cells nearest the route) for users who prefer lists over a grid; same actions.
One-switch. Auto-scan columns then rows (0.5 to 2 s adjustable), press to select, then scan the tray. A switch press also pauses the scan inside the radial menu. Pause and the next-wave card are scan stops one and two. Switch Access and keyboards: arrow keys move the cursor one nav cell, Enter places, Tab cycles tower, Escape cancels. Targets: every control carries a role, state and action label in EN and ES.
