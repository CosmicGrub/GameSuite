Why not jump straight to real 3D
Bot Bastion is a fixed-node diorama: 12 to 22 build nodes, no free camera needed.
The five proof models are 188 to 752 triangles; a whole arena is about 35k. Triangles are not the cost; engine size, lifecycle, TalkBack and thermals are.
What carries over from Phase 1 to Phase 2
The glTF models, palette, animations list, camera and picking maths, settings, event log and tests.
Only the draw back-end changes (SpriteIsoRenderer to FilamentRenderer behind one interface).
Fallback chain
3D Real -> 3D baked -> 2.5D -> Flat, automatic and visible as a chip.
Low-RAM devices and kid profiles never see the heavy pack by default.
