Bot Bastion: a Tower Defence mode in the spirit of a space bot-defence classic
Owner request: play like the 2D space tower defence on Wii U, then expand. We match its rules and structure (researched, mostly confident) with original art, names, sounds and maps. Numbers are ours.

Bot Bastion, Pebble Ring, wave 18
Original identity
Setting. You are the last crew of a drifting relay station, the Core, defending it with salvaged bots from rock-ships called Drifters. Neon on deep violet, drawn clean and top-down.
Currency. Scrap. Bots. Picket, Lobber, Haze, Skiff, Tender, plus Aegis shield. Tiers. Cadet, Captain, Galactic Hero, Insanity, Endless.
