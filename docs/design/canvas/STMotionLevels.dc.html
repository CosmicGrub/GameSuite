Redlines: motion levels

Numbers match dots on the boards.
1
Calm Opacity cross-fades only, 640 ms. No particles, no travel, no camera, no squash. One light haptic tick. Reduced Motion always lands here.S
2
Standard Eased moves, 900 ms, 8 specks, 0.95 landing squash, up to 4 percent overshoot. The default.S
3
Lively Spring motion, 1100 ms, 18 sparks plus 6 streaks, 1.22 stretch and 0.78 squash, 12 percent banner overshoot, 3 haptic pulses. Never flashes more than 3 times a second.M
4
Game speed Slow multiplies every duration by 1.5, Fast by 0.6. Easing and particle counts stay, except Fast drops the third bounce and half the particles.S
5
Reduced Motion Wins over the level setting. Applies Calm timing at the chosen game speed. Pauses ambient loops and 3D camera moves; static art style is untouched. Set in Settings, Motion, with the same three words everywhere.S
6
Budget guard A shared monitor steps the motion level down one step before the art style falls back, so the game stays pretty and just calmer.M
