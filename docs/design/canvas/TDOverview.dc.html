Tower Defence redesign: from eight fixed pads to an open battlefield
Today the game is one tower type on 8 fixed circles beside a polyline. The redesign keeps the engine's wave, gold, lives and difficulty rules and replaces the map layer, art and feel.

Open, not on rails
The player decides the defence. The game only protects one promise: enemies always have a way to the castle.
