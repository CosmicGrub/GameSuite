Gem set and specials

Colour and shape together. Each kind has a unique silhouette and an Okabe-Ito colour, so colour is never the only cue.S

Marks row. Optional inner mark (dot, bar, plus, ring, wave, slash, dots) for colour-blind mode; also shown in the greyscale test.S

3D gems. Facets lit from the top left, baked as sprites; 12 facets or fewer so the software fallback stays cheap.L

Special art. Overlay silhouettes (petals, arrows, double ring, colour wheel) keep each special readable at 32 px.M
5
Make diagram. The match shape is shown in the rules sheet and tutorial; the swap cell gets the special.S
6
Fire diagram. Reused as the hint overlay in Journey levels.S
7
Combo table. Ten pairs, all deterministic; scores and cues in EVENTS.md (engine team).M
8
Palette table. Contrast of every gem against the board is at least 3:1 (checked in the generator).S
