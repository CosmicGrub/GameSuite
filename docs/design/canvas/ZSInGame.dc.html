Bot Bastion: the in-game screen
Landscape arena, top-down. Scrap, Score, Core integrity and speed up top; five bots plus Aegis, Specials and Sell along the bottom; upgrade panel opens on the right. Original art; mechanics matched in structure to the researched source (see RESEARCH.md).
Mid-wave: Crosswind, Captain, wave 12 of 30

Redlines

Mid-wave, Captain difficulty
1
Wave / goal. Wave counter with the goal for the chosen difficulty (Endless shows no goal).S
2
Scrap. The single currency. Earned per kill, plus a bonus at wave end. Spent on bots, upgrades, Aegis.S
3
Score and multiplier. Score Boost special shows its live multiplier chip beside the score.S
4
Core integrity + Aegis shield. The base is the thing you protect. Aegis adds a blue shield segment that is spent first.M
5
Speed and pause. 1x / 2x / 3x and a big pause. Pause is instant and building while paused is allowed.M
6
Build nodes. Dashed circles are the spots you can build on. Filled = occupied. 12 to 22 per map.M
7
Lanes. Enemies fly glowing lanes toward the Core. Red rings mark where they enter.M
8
Bot with range ring. Selected bot: solid ring, stats and target priority. Lobber also shows its dashed blind spot.M
9
Upgrade panel. Power, Range, Rate and a before / after value. Target priority chips. Upgrade and Sell buttons.L
10
Weapon bar. Five bots with costs; dimmed when you cannot afford one. Keys 1 to 5 on keyboards.L
11
Aegis. Buy Core shield. The price rises 25 percent every time you buy it.M
12
Specials. Four earned items: two multipliers (stack) and two rare rescues. Tap to use.M
