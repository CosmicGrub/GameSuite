## What the votes say (page 1)
1. Cooperative deduction tops the list by votes (Outfoxed, 21), and cooperative games are about a quarter of the items.
2. Dexterity is the biggest family: Loopin' Louie, Animal Upon Animal, Rhino Hero (x2), ICECOOL, Coconuts, Dragon's Breath are 7 of 25 and 9 of the top 20 slots. On a touchscreen these become flick, stack and balance games with simple physics. This is the largest unbuilt opportunity in GameSuite.
3. Matching and speed (Spot it!) is the third most popular and the cheapest to build.
4. Tile-placement "first" versions (Dragomino, My First Carcassonne, Carcassonne) show kids can handle placement strategy with scoring kept simple.
5. A commenter notes that the favourites at age 5 (Loopin' Louie, Animal Upon Animal, Spot it!) are still played at 9 and are fun for adults too. Design for a long tail, not just age 5.
6. Votes follow the curator's own forum-mined list, so they reflect one community's taste, not a market. Treat as a signal, not proof.
